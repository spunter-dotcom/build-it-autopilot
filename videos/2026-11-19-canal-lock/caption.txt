No pumps. No engines. Just two gates and the water. 🚣💧
In 984, a Song engineer put two gates more than 50 paces apart, and boats rose 4 or 5 feet without ever leaving the water. Centuries later, Leonardo da Vinci may have drawn the V-shaped gates still used today. Would you have let the water do the lifting? 👇
