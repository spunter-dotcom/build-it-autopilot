# Sources: Building the Colosseum (2026-11-15)

Wikipedia and Britannica could not be fetched in this run, so every fact below comes from the-colosseum.net (a long-running reference site on the amphitheatre). Wording in the script is softened where the source is.

| Fact used | Source wording | URL |
|---|---|---|
| Built on the site of the lake of Nero's palace (script: "an emperor's private lake") | Nero "had his 'Domus Aurea' (Golden Palace)"; the site was "on the shore of a little Nero's lake" | https://the-colosseum.net/wp/en/echoes-from-the-colosseum/ |
| The lake site was drained and dug down to clay | "After the area was completely drained, the excavation started"; it reached "the clay bed of the lake" | https://the-colosseum.net/wp/en/foundations/ |
| Oval ring foundation of concrete, over 12 m thick | "an elliptical ring was excavated, 31 metres wide, 6 metres deep, with a perimeter of 530 metres", filled with "Roman cement ... mixed with coarse crushed stones", then "raised for a further 6 metres, so that the thickness of this enormous doughnut is over 12 metres" | https://the-colosseum.net/wp/en/foundations/ |
| Travertine, held with iron clamps | "Over 100.000 cubic metres of travertine stone were used"; "300 tons of metal were used for the iron clamps" | https://the-colosseum.net/wp/en/numbers/ |
| Travertine came about 20 km from near Tivoli (caption) | "A road about 20 km long was built to transport the travertine stones from the quarries near Tivoli" | https://the-colosseum.net/wp/en/numbers/ |
| 80 arches (so 80 piers) per level; three arcaded storeys and a plain fourth | "There were 80 arches on every floor"; "3 floors of arcades and a 4th storey with windows" | https://the-colosseum.net/wp/en/numbers/ |
| Seating carried on sloping vaults | "'rampant vaults' for the cavea and seat support" | https://the-colosseum.net/wp/en/echoes-from-the-colosseum/ |
| About 50,000 spectators | "The ancient capacity is calculated between 50.000 and 75.000 spectators" | https://the-colosseum.net/wp/en/numbers/ |
| 240 wooden masts for the awning (velarium) | "sockets for 240 wooden beams which supported the awning (velarium)" | https://the-colosseum.net/wp/en/numbers/ |
| Opened in the year 80 | Titus "dedicated it in 80 A.D." (the same page also mentions a dedication by Vespasian in 79; the script uses the 80 date for the opening games) | https://the-colosseum.net/wp/en/echoes-from-the-colosseum/ |

Not used because unsourced in this run: the sailors who worked the awning, exit times, the 80,000 capacity figure, "no mortar".
"Travertine, a hard, pale limestone" is a plain-English gloss of the stone's name, not a sourced measurement.
