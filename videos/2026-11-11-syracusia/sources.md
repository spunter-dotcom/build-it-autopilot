# Sources: "You built Archimedes' giant ship" (2026-11-11)

Owner-assigned topic: the Syracusia, built for Hiero II of Syracuse under Archimedes. Almost everything we know comes from one ancient text: Athenaeus, *Deipnosophists* V (206d–209e), quoting a writer called Moschion. Details are therefore phrased as "ancient writers say" where they sound most extraordinary, and modern size estimates (55 m vs 110 m) are left out because they are not in the ancient text and sources disagree.

Pages used:
- [A] Athenaeus, *Deipnosophists* Book V, Loeb translation (C. B. Gulick, 1928), on LacusCurtius: https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Athenaeus/5C*.html
- [W] World History Encyclopedia, *Syracusia* (Stella Nenova): https://www.worldhistory.org/Syracusia/
