No springs. No batteries. Just a bowl and a drip. ⏳💧
Around 1400 BC, Egyptians carved a stone bowl with 12 columns of hour marks, one for every month. About 250 BC, Ctesibius of Alexandria flipped the idea so water flowed in and lifted a float with a pointer. Would you have thought to flip it? 👇
