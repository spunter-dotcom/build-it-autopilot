No machines. No engines. Just stone, timber and a giant screw. 🫒🪵
Roman olive presses could be up to 12 metres long and weigh many tonnes, and villagers built them from local stone and wood. Some lever-and-screw presses were still working in the 1950s. Would you have thought of the screw? 👇
