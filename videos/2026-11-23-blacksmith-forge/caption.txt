No factories. No machines. Just fire, air and a hammer. 🔥🔨
Smiths pushed air through charcoal with a bellows until iron reached a white "snowball heat," then fused two bars into one with a few hammer blows. Punching a single hole could take up to 3 reheats. Would you have thought of the bellows? 👇
