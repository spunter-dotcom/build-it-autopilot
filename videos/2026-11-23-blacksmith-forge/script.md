# You built a blacksmith's forge

How did a village mend its plows before factories? It needed a blacksmith. So you build a forge.
So you lay bricks into a hearth, and pack earth inside so the fire can't spread. You heap charcoal on top and light it.
But charcoal alone just smolders. Iron needs far more heat. So you build a bellows: two boards, leather sides and a pipe into the coals. Squeeze it, and air roars through the fire.
This is a bellows.
Now you push an iron bar into the coals. It glows blood red, then orange, then almost white. It never melts. It just goes soft.
But soft iron needs something solid to push against. So you set a heavy steel block on a stump beside the hearth.
This is an anvil.
You grip the bar with tongs and start hammering. Draw it out, bend it, punch a hole. When it cools and stiffens, it goes back in the fire.
Heat two bars to white heat, strike them together, and they fuse into one. That is a forge weld.
Last, you plunge a hot steel blade into the water trough. Hiss. Now it is hard.
Congratulations. You have built a blacksmith's forge.
