# Sources: Sculpting the statue of David

- The Carrara block was abandoned by Agostino di Duccio, then by Antonio Rossellino, and lay in the cathedral workshop yard for another 25 years; assigned to Michelangelo in 1501; 40 men took four days to move the statue half a mile. https://news.artnet.com/art-world/art-bites-michelangelo-david-roof-2455527
- Michelangelo was 26 in August 1501; the statue is 17 feet tall; it was moved hanging from ropes inside a wooden cage pulled on greased logs. https://www.mentalfloss.com/art/michelangelos-david-duomo-roof
- The block was tall but narrow, with tiny holes and visible veins. https://www.contexttravel.com/blog/articles/ten-facts-about-the-statue-of-david
- Height 5.17 m (17 ft); made 1501-1504. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_(Michelangelo)
