No radiators. No electricity. Just fire and a floor on stacks of tiles. 🏛️🔥
Romans lifted bath floors onto stacks of tiles, topped them with 60 cm "two-foot" tiles and let furnace heat flow underneath. At the Baths of Caracalla, 50 furnaces burned ten tons of wood a day. Would you have thought of heating the floor? 👇
