No pumps. No engines. Just a river and a wheel. 🌊🪣
The biggest noria in Hama, Syria, built in 1361, is 21 m across, carries 120 boxes on its rim and could lift 200,000 litres of water an hour. Would you have thought to let the river do the lifting? 👇
