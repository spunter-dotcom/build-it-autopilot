No cement. No factories. Just rock, fire and water. 🔥🪨
Kilns baked limestone at 900 °C or more for three days, and a single batch could make 25 to 30 tonnes of lime. People were already using lime around 7500 BC. Would you have thought to burn a rock? 👇
