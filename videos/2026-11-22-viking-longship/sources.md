# Sources: Viking longship

- Gokstad ship: about 23.8 m long, 5.1 m beam, built of oak, timber felled around 890 AD; 16 planks (strakes) per side; 16 oar rooms, so 32 oars; rivets about 125-180 mm apart; square sail about 110 square metres; side (quarter) rudder; found 1880. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gokstad_ship
- Clinker (lapstrake) build: planks overlap and are fastened with iron rivets; keel and stems first, then planks, then ribs inserted inside the planking ("shell building"); planks split from oak logs, no saws in northern Europe until the 13th century. https://www.worldhistory.org/Viking_Ships
- Broad axe rather than saw; single side rudder on the right, the "steering board" (starboard) side; Roskilde longship draft only about three feet, about 119 ft long, room for at least 72 oars; replicas up to 14 knots; Gokstad built around AD 890. https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/nova/ancient/viking-ships.html
