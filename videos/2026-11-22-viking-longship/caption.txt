No saws. No steel hull. Just split oak and iron rivets. ⚓🪓
The Gokstad ship, built around AD 890, is about 23.8 m long with 16 overlapping planks a side, holes for 32 oars and a square sail of about 110 square metres. Would you have trusted a hull this thin on the open sea? 👇
